## 6. UNIQUE MECHANISM (UMP + UMS)

### UMP — The Fiber Starvation Effect
Your gut bacteria are starving. American fiber intake has dropped 28% since 1909 and now averages just 16g/day — less than half the recommended 25–38g. Without fiber, the beneficial bacteria that regulate digestion, fermentation, and appetite die off and are replaced by gas-producing bacteria. This creates a three-part cascade:
1. **Poor motility** — not enough SCFAs to keep digestion moving → backup, sluggishness
2. **Chaotic fermentation** — wrong bacteria produce excess gas → daily belly distension
3. **False hunger** — disrupted gut-brain signals → cravings that aren't real hunger

### UMS — Feed, Don't Add
BloatReset delivers five targeted prebiotic fibers (agave inulin, GOS, guar gum, acacia gum, flaxseed) that reach the colon and selectively feed the beneficial bacteria already there. As these bacteria recover, they restore SCFA production → motility normalizes → fermentation calms → appetite signals correct. This is not a probiotic (adding bacteria) or an enzyme (masking symptoms). It's feeding the foundation.
