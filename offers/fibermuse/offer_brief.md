## Ingredient Breakdown (Plain-Language)

Use this section to write benefit bullets without making disease claims.

### BloatReset (Prebiotic Complex — 4251-PREBIOTC)
**Prebiotic / fiber sources (Proprietary Blend 800 mg):**
- **Agave inulin:** classic prebiotic fiber that supports beneficial bacteria and gut balance over time.
- **Galacto-oligosaccharides (GOS):** targeted prebiotic often used to support the growth of friendly bacteria.
- **Guar gum + Acaia gum:** soluble fibers that may support digestive comfort and regularity by feeding beneficial bacteria and helping stool consistency.
- **Flaxseed:** fiber + plant compounds that support digestive comfort and satiety.

**What to expect:** some users may feel temporary gas/bloating when starting prebiotics—consider “start low / build up” guidance in your usage copy.

- **Dandelion root (50 mg) + Chicory root (50 mg):** traditional herbs used for digestive and liver support.
