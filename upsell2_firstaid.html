What's Inside the Training Vault

Five modules that could mean the difference between life and death. The same techniques taught to combat medics — adapted for responsible gun owners.

Module 1: Stopping the Bleed — Tourniquet Mastery

60% of preventable combat deaths are from blood loss. Learn the exact same tourniquet technique taught to Special Forces medics. Proper placement. Correct tightness. When to use one and when NOT to.

Your wife is bleeding from her leg. You have 90 seconds. Do you know what to do?

Module 2: Chest Wounds — The Silent Killer

A penetrating chest wound collapses a lung in minutes. Learn to identify, seal, and stabilize. The difference between an open and tension pneumothorax — and why getting this wrong kills faster than the bullet.

A chest wound makes a sucking sound. If you don't know what that means, it's already too late.

Module 3: Wound Packing & Pressure — When Tourniquets Can't Reach

Neck. Armpit. Groin. Some wounds are in places a tourniquet can't go. Learn direct pressure, wound packing with hemostatic gauze, and the technique that keeps blood inside the body until help arrives.

Module 4: Calling 911 — What to Say (and What NEVER to Say)

The wrong words on a 911 call can land you in prison. The right words save lives AND protect you legally. Exact scripts. Exact sequence. What the dispatcher needs and what your lawyer needs you to NOT say.

"I shot someone" vs. "Someone's been shot" — those 2 words change your entire legal future.

Module 5: Managing Shock — Keeping Them Alive Until Help Arrives

Even after you stop the bleeding, shock kills. Pale skin. Rapid breathing. Confusion. Learn the exact positioning, temperature management, and monitoring that keeps the body from shutting down.

Even after you stop the bleed, shock can finish the job. Do you know the signs?
