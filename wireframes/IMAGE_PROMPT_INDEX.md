## GENERATION NOTES

**Style consistency:** All images should maintain the navy (#1a1a2e) and red (#cc0000) brand palette. Photorealistic style throughout. Avoid cartoon/illustration unless specified (infographics).

**AI tool recommendation:** Midjourney v6+ or DALL-E 3 for photorealistic scenes. Use `--ar 16:9` for banner/landscape formats, `--ar 4:3` for product mockups, `--ar 1:1` for badges/avatars.

**Aspect ratios by size:**
- 800×600 = 4:3 (product mockups)
- 680×400 = 17:10 ≈ 16:9 (scenario scenes)
- 680×380 = ~16:9 (chapter cards)
- 1000×300-450 = ultra-wide (banners/infographics)
- 500×350 = ~3:2 (bonus items)
- 56×56 = 1:1 (avatars)

**Total unique images needed: 37**
- Shared assets: 2 (payment badges + guarantee seal)
- Upsell 1: 13 (1 mockup + 10 scenarios + 1 consequences panel + 4 avatars optional)
- Upsell 2: 9 (1 mockup + 1 fear scene + 1 timeline + 5 modules + 2 bonuses)
- Upsell 3: 12 (1 mockup + 1 fear scene + 1 infographic + 7 chapters + 1 bonus + 1 banner)
- Upsell 4: 6 (3 mini cards + 1 hero bundle + 1 emotional scene + 1 final CTA)
